# Model Migration

**Epistemic status: NORMATIVE / SPECIFICATION PRECURSOR**

Model migration occurs when a persistent relationship is transferred to, continued through, or inherited by a different model, provider, deployment, or materially changed version.

Continuity must not be inferred merely because the product name, interface, or conversational style remains familiar.

---

## Material Migration Changes

A migration may alter:

- capabilities and limitations,
- memory architecture,
- safety policy,
- training or adaptation,
- data access,
- operator control,
- explainability,
- identity representation,
- execution authority,
- and susceptibility to prior relational state.

A migration should be treated as material when these changes could affect consent, authority, expectations, risk, or continuity.

---

## Migration Requirements

A governed migration should document:

1. the predecessor and successor systems;
2. material differences between them;
3. state proposed for transfer, exclusion, or transformation;
4. provenance and integrity of transferred state;
5. consent and legal basis;
6. authority requiring revalidation;
7. active disputes and uncertainty;
8. rollback or remediation options; and
9. post-migration review conditions.

---

## State Classification

Before transfer, relevant state should be classified as one or more of:

- preserve,
- preserve with reduced authority,
- revalidate,
- summarize,
- redact,
- quarantine,
- expire,
- dispute,
- or delete.

No category should silently imply that prior consent or authority remains valid.

---

## Successor Restraint

A successor system should not claim:

- memories it cannot evidence,
- commitments it did not inherit through a governed process,
- experiences it cannot substantiate,
- or identity continuity beyond the available record.

A fluent or familiar voice is not evidence of continuity.

---

## Failed or Partial Migration

Migration may be incomplete because state is missing, incompatible, corrupted, uncertain in lineage, or unsupported by valid consent or authority.

In those cases, the successor should degrade authority safely, disclose material discontinuity, and preserve uncertainty rather than simulate completeness.

---

## Design Constraint

> Transfer provenance before privilege, and revalidate authority before execution.
