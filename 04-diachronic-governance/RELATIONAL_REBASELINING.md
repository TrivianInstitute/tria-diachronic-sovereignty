# Relational Re-Baselining

**Epistemic status: NORMATIVE / SPECIFICATION PRECURSOR**

Relational re-baselining establishes a new governed reference state after material change.

It allows a relationship to continue without making its earliest state permanently authoritative.

---

## Possible Triggers

Re-baselining may be appropriate after:

- a material identity or role change,
- model migration,
- significant capability change,
- repair following harm,
- extended dormancy,
- consent drift,
- a changed purpose or institutional context,
- revision of important shared claims,
- or a participant request.

No universal interval or trigger threshold is asserted here.

---

## Baseline Contents

A new baseline should identify, where applicable:

- current participants and roles,
- consent and permissions,
- active authority,
- current shared claims,
- disputes,
- uncertainty,
- lifecycle state,
- inherited obligations,
- excluded or expired assumptions,
- provenance links,
- and review conditions.

---

## What Re-Baselining Does Not Do

Re-baselining does not automatically:

- erase harm,
- extinguish obligations,
- resolve disputes,
- validate prior interpretations,
- transfer authority,
- fabricate continuity,
- or prevent later revision.

A new baseline governs the present relationship while preserving relevant accountability.

---

## Procedure

A governed re-baselining process should:

1. identify the material change;
2. preserve provenance to the predecessor state;
3. surface inherited assumptions;
4. classify them as accepted, disputed, expired, or uncertain;
5. obtain any required consent;
6. record exclusions and unresolved matters; and
7. define future review conditions.

---

## Re-Baselining and Repair

A new baseline is not proof that repair has occurred.

Where harm or breach preceded re-baselining, the record should preserve remediation duties, residual effects, and unresolved claims.

---

## Design Constraint

> A relationship may begin again from a new place without pretending that it has no history.
