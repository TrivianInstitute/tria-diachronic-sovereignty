# Re-Consent

**Epistemic status: NORMATIVE / SPECIFICATION PRECURSOR**

Re-consent is a governed renewal or revision of authorization after material relational change.

It is not a ritual confirmation screen. It must restore meaningful choice.

## When Re-Consent May Be Required

Candidate triggers include:

- expanded capability or authority;
- a new purpose for retained information;
- model, operator, or institutional migration;
- entry into a higher-consequence domain;
- substantial identity or role change;
- prolonged dormancy;
- detected consent drift;
- changed reversibility or risk; or
- participant request.

## Minimum Requirements

A re-consent process should make legible:

- what changed;
- why the change matters;
- what prior authorization exists;
- what new or continued authorization is requested;
- what will happen if consent is withheld;
- which effects are reversible;
- how consent may later be narrowed or revoked; and
- the effective scope and duration.

## Possible Outcomes

Re-consent may result in:

- renewal without change;
- narrower permission;
- expanded permission;
- conditional permission;
- time-limited permission;
- refusal;
- pause pending clarification;
- transition to reduced engagement; or
- dissolution.

Refusal must not be silently translated into consent or hostility.

## Granularity

Authorization should be separable where consequences differ.

A participant may permit memory retention while refusing execution authority, or permit research use while refusing personalization.

## Procedural Consent

Explicit confirmation may not be practical for every low-consequence transition. A visible, contestable procedure may be used where appropriate, but it must not obscure material changes or override applicable law.

## Record

A re-consent record should reference:

- prior consent;
- disclosed material changes;
- scope;
- responsible actors;
- decision;
- timestamp;
- expiry or review condition; and
- disputes or accessibility accommodations.

## Design Principle

> **Re-consent should renew agency, not merely renew permission.**
