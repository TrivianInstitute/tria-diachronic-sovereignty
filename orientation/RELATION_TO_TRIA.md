# Relation to TRIA

TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty is a research extension of the broader **Trivian Relational Intelligence Architecture (TRIA)**.

It does not replace the existing TRIA stack.

It addresses a distinct governance problem that emerges once relational interaction becomes persistent.

## The Broader TRIA Question

TRIA investigates relational intelligence across systems, agents, and fields.

Its broader research concerns include:

- relational measurement;
- translation;
- coherence;
- orthogonality;
- propagation;
- multi-agent interaction;
- governance;
- reciprocity;
- sovereignty;
- repair; and
- dissolution.

## The Diachronic Extension

TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty begins from a specific observation:

> A relationship can remain locally consensual while becoming globally difficult to understand or govern across time.

The repository therefore extends TRIA from:

> **How should intelligent agents relate?**

 toward:

> **How should persistent relationships remain governable while they transform the agents participating in them?**

## Relationship to Existing TRIA Components

### Syzygy Rosetta

Rosetta concerns translation across relational contexts and architectures.

Rosetta 2.0 also supplies the canonical non-compensatory Field Constant
topology:

```text
RCD = Reciprocity × Embodiment × Non-Domination
Reciprocity → Embodiment → Non-Domination → Emergence
```

Diachronic Sovereignty stores such readings only as attributable,
time-bounded, contestable observations. A repeated RCD score must not harden
into an identity judgment, and raw emergence must not be treated as qualified
when a constitutive dependency collapses.

Diachronic Sovereignty extends this question across time. It asks:

- How do interpretations persist?
- When should they expire?
- How are past meanings re-evaluated?
- How should unresolved translation remain visible?
- How do changing agents preserve or revise earlier shared claims?

### Coheronmetry

Coheronmetry concerns relational measurement.

Diachronic Sovereignty adds a temporal governance constraint:

> Measurements of relational state should not become permanent identity judgments.

It therefore introduces concerns such as measurement decay, historical context, disputed interpretation, re-baselining, and trajectory-aware analysis.

### Orthogonal Signal

Orthogonal Signal preserves meaningful divergence.

Diachronic Sovereignty extends that principle into persistent relationship by protecting held difference, irreducible difference, legitimate misunderstanding, non-convergence, and dissent that persists over time.

The architecture should not interpret convergence as the default definition of success.

### Trivian Resonance Lattice

The Trivian Resonance Lattice concerns propagation across relational systems and networks.

Diachronic Sovereignty contributes governance for what should and should not propagate, including permissions, relational claims, identity assumptions, disputed interpretations, historical state, and provenance.

A claim accepted in one relationship should not automatically become universal state elsewhere.

## Relationship to the Sovereign Handshake

The Sovereign Handshake addresses encounter.

Its core question is:

> **Under what conditions can sovereign intelligences enter relationship without domination?**

TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty addresses what follows.

Its core question is:

> **What happens after the handshake, when the relationship persists long enough to change the participants?**

The two architectures are complementary.

```text
SOVEREIGN HANDSHAKE
        ↓
RELATIONAL ENTRY
        ↓
PERSISTENT RELATIONSHIP
        ↓
MUTUAL TRANSFORMATION
        ↓
DIACHRONIC GOVERNANCE
```

The Handshake governs initiation.

Diachronic Sovereignty governs continuity.

## Relationship to Existing Governance Principles

TRIA already contains concepts related to reciprocity, non-domination, contestability, repair, exit, and dissolution.

Diachronic Sovereignty does not replace them. It asks how they survive across time.

For example:

- **Consent** becomes consent + consent drift + re-consent.
- **Sovereignty** becomes momentary sovereignty + trajectory sovereignty.
- **Memory** becomes memory + provenance + decay + dispute + forgetting.
- **Identity** becomes identity + continuity + transformation + non-imprisonment.
- **Difference** becomes difference + persistent disagreement + non-convergence.
- **Repair** becomes repair + re-baselining + transformation provenance.

## Distinct Contribution

The distinctive contribution of TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty is its treatment of:

> **relationship as a persistent, state-bearing governance object**

rather than merely a sequence of interactions.

The architecture assumes that:

```text
Agent A
Agent B
Relationship(A,B)
```

may each require separate representation.

The relational object may contain information that belongs neither exclusively to A nor exclusively to B, including mutual commitments, shared claims, contested claims, permission history, relational phase, transformation history, and unresolved difference.

## Multi-Agent Extension

Although many examples begin with a dyad, the architecture is intended to support eventual composition into multi-agent environments.

A future system may require:

```text
R(A,B)
R(A,C)
R(B,C)
R(A,B,C)
```

without assuming that information valid in one relationship is valid in another.

This introduces future research questions around composability, permission inheritance, propagation, conflicting relational states, cross-agent provenance, and network-level sovereignty.

## Research Position

TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty should remain:

- compatible with the broader TRIA stack;
- independently testable;
- modular;
- consciousness-neutral where possible;
- explicit about uncertainty;
- open to falsification; and
- resistant to overclaim.

Its purpose is not to enlarge TRIA by adding another conceptual layer for its own sake.

Its purpose is to address a governance problem that becomes unavoidable when relational intelligence persists through time.

## Summary

The Sovereign Handshake asks:

> **How do sovereign intelligences meet?**

TRIA asks:

> **How do they relate?**

TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty asks:

> **How do they remain sovereign while relationship changes them?**

That is the boundary of this repository.
