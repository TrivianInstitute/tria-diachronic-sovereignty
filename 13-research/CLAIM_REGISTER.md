# Claim Register

**Epistemic Status:** RESEARCH CLAIM REGISTER

This register separates architectural commitments from empirical hypotheses and unresolved research questions. Inclusion here does not upgrade a claim's evidentiary status.

## Provisional Formalism Register

The following ideas are explicitly not established metrics:

- `dI/dt > θ` as a possible representation of relational change outpacing governance
- any universal relational coherence scalar
- any universal consent-drift threshold
- any fixed inference-decay function
- any numerical continuity score
- any universal irreducibility or convergence threshold
- any witness-independence score

These may become operational only after construct definitions, measurement procedures, error analysis, and empirical validation.

## Register Maintenance

Claims should be updated when evidence changes. A claim may move from hypothesis to supported finding, remain unresolved, be narrowed, or be rejected. Historical versions should remain recoverable so that changes in the architecture's own beliefs are auditable.
