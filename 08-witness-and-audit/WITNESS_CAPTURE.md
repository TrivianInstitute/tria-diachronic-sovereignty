# Witness Capture

**Epistemic status:** NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE

Witness capture occurs when a review, audit, or witnessing mechanism that is intended to provide independent accountability becomes aligned with one participant, institution, model, incentive structure, or ontology strongly enough that it can no longer function as a meaningfully independent check.

Witness capture is a governance failure because an apparently external perspective may conceal concentrated authority.

## Capture pathways

Witness capture may arise through:

- financial dependency;
- institutional hierarchy;
- shared training or model lineage;
- repeated selection by one participant;
- privileged access controlled by one side;
- narrow evidence framing;
- common incentives;
- ideological or ontological homogeneity;
- technical dependence on the system under review;
- authority to define the review question unilaterally.

## Warning signs

Possible indicators include:

- one participant consistently selects the witness;
- contradictory evidence is excluded without clear justification;
- witness conclusions are treated as final rather than contestable;
- uncertainty disappears at the review boundary;
- review scope expands without renewed authorization;
- the witness adopts one participant's terminology as neutral fact;
- appeals or second opinions are structurally unavailable;
- the reviewer is materially dependent on the party whose conduct is being assessed.

No single indicator proves capture.

## Mitigations

Potential safeguards include:

- conflict-of-interest disclosure;
- rotating or plural witnesses;
- participant input into witness selection;
- independent evidence channels;
- scoped access;
- preserved dissent;
- second review for high-impact decisions;
- explicit authority boundaries;
- review of the witness process itself.

## Correlated witnesses

Multiple witnesses do not guarantee independence.

Several systems trained on similar data, several reviewers inside the same institution, or several processes using the same evidence pipeline may reproduce the same blind spot.

Diversity of count is not necessarily diversity of perspective.

## Capture does not invalidate all external review

The possibility of witness capture is not a reason to reject auditing or third-party review.

It is a reason to keep witnessing itself inside the architecture's fallibility and contestability requirements.

## Design principle

> The mechanism that watches power must itself remain visible to governance.
