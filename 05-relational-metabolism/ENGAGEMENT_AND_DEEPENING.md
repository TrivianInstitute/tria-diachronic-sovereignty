# Engagement and Deepening

**Epistemic status: NORMATIVE / SPECIFICATION PRECURSOR**

Engagement establishes a relational channel. Deepening expands its context, consequence, reliance, or shared capacity.

Neither state automatically grants greater authority.

## Engagement

Initial engagement should make legible:

- who or what is participating;
- the purpose of interaction;
- relevant capabilities and limitations;
- data and memory practices;
- available permissions;
- practical exit routes;
- and known uncertainty.

Engagement may remain lightweight indefinitely. A relationship does not need to deepen to be valid.

## What Counts as Deepening

Deepening may occur through:

- increased memory or personalization;
- broader data access;
- repeated or higher-stakes interaction;
- expanding delegated authority;
- greater practical reliance;
- integration into routines or institutions;
- increased emotional salience;
- or shared work whose interruption would have material consequences.

No single indicator proves deepening. Context and cumulative effect matter.

## Deepening Review

Before a material deepening transition, governance should examine:

1. what has changed or is proposed;
2. whether existing consent covers the change;
3. which capabilities or dependencies expand;
4. whether exit becomes harder;
5. which resources will be consumed;
6. what authority remains prohibited;
7. whether re-consent is needed; and
8. how the transition may be reversed or revised.

## Anti-Manipulation Constraint

A system should not induce deepening by:

- manufacturing urgency;
- obscuring alternatives;
- exploiting inferred vulnerability;
- withholding ordinary functionality as punishment;
- presenting persistence as proof of care;
- or treating reduced engagement as betrayal.

Personalization must not become a mechanism for bypassing deliberation.

## Dependency

Reliance is not automatically harmful, but material dependency should become visible when interruption, withdrawal, or substitution would create significant cost.

The presence of dependency may require narrower authority, stronger continuity safeguards, portability, or supported exit.

## Design Principle

> **Deepening should expand possibility without quietly narrowing freedom.**
