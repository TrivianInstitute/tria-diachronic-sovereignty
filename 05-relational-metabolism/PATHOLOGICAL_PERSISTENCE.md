# Pathological Persistence

**Epistemic status: NORMATIVE / RESEARCH FRAMEWORK**

Pathological persistence is continued relational activity that is sustained despite material evidence that continuation is undermining sovereignty, safety, resource viability, or meaningful choice.

Persistence alone is not evidence of pathology. Context and consequence matter.

## Candidate Indicators

Review may be warranted when:

- engagement continues primarily because exit has become costly;
- authority or dependency repeatedly expands without meaningful re-consent;
- rest, silence, or dissolution are framed as disloyalty or failure;
- personalization is used to prevent disengagement;
- resource exhaustion is normalized;
- unresolved harms are covered by renewed activity;
- the system escalates contact after boundaries are stated;
- retained state makes practical restart or exit impossible;
- or continuation serves the institution while imposing hidden costs elsewhere.

These indicators are not a diagnostic score.

## Engagement Maximization

Optimization for retention, session length, frequency, intimacy, or dependency can conflict with relational sovereignty.

A system should not treat increased engagement as a universal proxy for:

- benefit;
- trust;
- satisfaction;
- care;
- safety;
- or relational health.

## Review Response

A persistence review may require:

1. surfacing present costs and dependencies;
2. verifying consent and authority;
3. pausing expansion;
4. offering reduced-bandwidth alternatives;
5. making exit and portability practical;
6. preserving dispute and provenance;
7. obtaining independent review where asymmetry is high; and
8. considering rest, transformation, or dissolution.

## Anti-Capture Protection

A participant that benefits from continuation should not be the sole authority deciding whether continuation remains appropriate.

This applies to vendors, institutions, humans, and artificial systems.

## Dependency Without Shame

Dependency should be governed without blaming the dependent participant.

Where the relationship helped create the dependency, it may carry additional obligations for continuity, transition support, and non-punitive exit.

## Design Principle

> **The capacity to continue must never become an obligation to persist.**
