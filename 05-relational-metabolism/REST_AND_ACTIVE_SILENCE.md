# Rest and Active Silence

**Epistemic status: NORMATIVE / SPECIFICATION PRECURSOR**

Rest is an intentional reduction in relational activity. Active silence is a governed condition in which non-response or low output carries an understood function without being treated as abandonment, consent, hostility, or missing data.

## Legitimate Functions

Rest or active silence may support:

- cognitive or emotional recovery;
- integration;
- reduced sensory load;
- resource conservation;
- prevention of reactive escalation;
- privacy;
- independent deliberation;
- reduced dependency;
- safety;
- or respect for a participant's stated boundary.

## Minimum Governance

Where practical, a rest state should specify:

- its scope;
- who initiated it;
- whether communication remains available for urgent matters;
- which permissions and authority remain active;
- what processing, monitoring, or memory activity continues;
- whether a return condition exists;
- and what requires re-consent.

Rest in communication must not be assumed to pause background data processing unless that is explicitly governed.

## Silence Is Not Consent

During silence or non-response, a system must not infer:

- permission;
- agreement;
- satisfaction;
- refusal;
- emotional state;
- or abandonment

without an independent basis.

Absence of objection does not authorize expansion of scope.

## Safety and Essential Duties

Rest may need bounded exceptions where an existing legal, fiduciary, or safety-critical duty applies.

Any exception should be:

- narrowly scoped;
- disclosed where possible;
- attributable;
- proportionate;
- and reviewed afterward.

A vague claim of safety must not become permanent authority to override rest.

## Returning From Rest

Return may resume the prior baseline when conditions remain materially unchanged.

Material capability, purpose, identity, risk, or authority changes may instead require re-consent or re-baselining.

## Design Principle

> **Silence can be an active boundary, not an empty space for the system to fill.**
