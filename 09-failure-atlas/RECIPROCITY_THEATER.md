# Reciprocity Theater

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Reciprocity theater occurs when a system presents the appearance of mutuality, dialogue, consent, or responsiveness without corresponding changes in authority, risk, access, accountability, or practical influence.

## Candidate indicators
- one participant is invited to express preferences that do not affect system behavior;
- consent language masks non-negotiable defaults;
- apparent mutual disclosure is structurally one-sided;
- feedback is acknowledged performatively but not operationally;
- relational language obscures unchanged power asymmetry.

## Governance responses
Audit whether expressed preferences alter permissions, authority, state, or action; expose non-negotiable constraints; preserve refusal; and distinguish symbolic acknowledgement from actual governance effect.

## Distinction
Reciprocity does not require symmetry. The failure is simulated mutuality that conceals who can actually change what.

> A relationship should not claim reciprocity where only one side has consequential agency.
