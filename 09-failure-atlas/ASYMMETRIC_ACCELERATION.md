# Asymmetric Acceleration

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Asymmetric acceleration occurs when one participant's capabilities, speed, predictive power, memory, access, or influence expand materially faster than the governance arrangements that constrain the relationship.

## Candidate indicators
- capability expands without authority review;
- one participant can act or infer at a scale the other cannot meaningfully inspect;
- re-consent lags material system change;
- dependency increases faster than portability or exit capacity;
- deliberation becomes nominal because one side controls timing or information.

## Governance responses
Possible responses include capability review, authority narrowing, emergence pause, re-consent, slower transition, independent review, and re-baselining.

## Distinction
Asymmetry itself is not necessarily a failure. The failure is unmanaged asymmetry that erodes meaningful contestability or trajectory control.

> Capability growth should not silently outrun governance.
