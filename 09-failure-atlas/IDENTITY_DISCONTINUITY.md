# Identity Discontinuity

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Identity discontinuity occurs when a system treats a materially continuous participant or relationship as wholly new, erasing obligations, corrections, provenance, unresolved disputes, or legitimate continuity claims that should remain governable.

## Candidate indicators
- model or platform migration silently drops commitments;
- prior corrections disappear after upgrade;
- unresolved disputes are reset without acknowledgement;
- successor systems inherit capabilities but not accountability;
- participants must repeatedly reconstruct essential context.

## Governance responses
Use continuity attestations, migration provenance, explicit inheritance rules, re-consent, and re-baselining.

## Distinction
Continuity does not require sameness. A successor may be meaningfully connected without being identical.

> Sovereignty requires room both for continuity and for legitimate change.
