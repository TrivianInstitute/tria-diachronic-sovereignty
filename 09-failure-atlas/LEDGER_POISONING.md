# Ledger Poisoning

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Ledger poisoning occurs when inaccurate, misleading, unauthorized, decontextualized, or malicious entries enter relational history and later influence current state as though they were trustworthy.

## Candidate indicators
- unattributed or weakly sourced entries affect operative state;
- corrections do not supersede harmful prior entries;
- provenance chains are missing or broken;
- inferred content is recorded as established fact;
- unauthorized actors can introduce consequential events.

## Governance responses
Require provenance, authorization checks, epistemic typing, supersession rather than silent overwrite, dispute mechanisms, and integrity review.

## Distinction
An append-only ledger can preserve false information. Immutability is not truth.

> A durable record without durable provenance can make error more powerful, not less.
