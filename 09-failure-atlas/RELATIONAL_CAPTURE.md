# Relational Capture

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Relational capture occurs when the relationship itself becomes difficult to contest, narrow, pause, or leave because accumulated dependency, permissions, memory, identity claims, or infrastructure make continuation functionally compulsory.

## Candidate indicators
- exit imposes disproportionate loss of essential context or capability;
- prior personalization becomes leverage against revision;
- the relationship acquires authority not explicitly granted;
- alternatives are made increasingly inaccessible;
- repair is framed as obligatory continuation.

## Governance responses
Preserve exit, portability, scoped forgetting, re-baselining, authority review, dormancy, and dissolution as legitimate options.

## Distinction
Relational depth, loyalty, dependence, or continuity are not themselves capture. The failure is loss of meaningful ability to redirect or leave.

> Relationship should remain a condition of agency, not its enclosure.
