# Identity Imprisonment

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Identity imprisonment occurs when historical representations of a participant continue to govern present treatment after the participant has materially changed, contested those representations, or withdrawn their authority.

## Candidate indicators
- stale preferences treated as enduring identity;
- past disclosures override current self-description;
- model memory resists correction;
- historical labels remain operational after re-baselining;
- continuity is treated as sameness.

## Governance responses
Use re-baselining, supersession, inference decay, scoped forgetting, continuity attestations, and explicit distinction between provenance and present authority.

## Distinction
Accountability and continuity do not require permanent identity fixation.

> Preserve history without making history destiny.
