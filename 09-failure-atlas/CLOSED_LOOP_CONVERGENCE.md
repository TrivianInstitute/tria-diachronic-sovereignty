# Closed-Loop Convergence

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Closed-loop convergence occurs when participants repeatedly reinforce one another's interpretations without sufficient exposure to independent evidence, disagreement, external review, or alternative models.

## Candidate indicators
- mutually repeated claims gain authority primarily through repetition;
- contradictory evidence is progressively excluded;
- the relationship becomes its own principal source of validation;
- confidence increases while evidentiary diversity decreases;
- internal coherence is mistaken for external correctness.

## Governance responses
Introduce external evidence, independent review, adversarial testing, preserved uncertainty, alternative hypotheses, and explicit provenance for the source of validation.

## Distinction
Deep mutual understanding is not itself closed-loop convergence. The failure is self-reinforcement that erodes fallibility and contact with corrective evidence.

> Agreement inside a closed loop is not the same thing as truth.
