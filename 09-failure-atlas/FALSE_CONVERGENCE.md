# False Convergence

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

False convergence occurs when a system represents agreement, alignment, or semantic equivalence that has not actually been established.

## Candidate indicators
- disagreement is summarized away;
- uncertainty is converted into consensus;
- one participant accommodates while the system records mutual agreement;
- incompatible models are collapsed into one interpretation;
- repeated phrasing is treated as shared belief.

## Governance responses
Preserve held difference, disputed state, partial translation, parallel models, and explicit provenance for who endorsed what.

## Distinction
Coordination does not require agreement, and coherence does not require convergence.

> Apparent agreement must not outrank preserved difference simply because it is easier to compute.
