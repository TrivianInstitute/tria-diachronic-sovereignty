# Ontology Capture

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Ontology capture occurs when one participant's categories, concepts, or interpretive frame become the default structure through which the relationship represents reality, while alternative frames lose practical standing.

## Why it matters

Persistent systems accumulate vocabulary and assumptions. Over time, convenience can harden one ontology into infrastructure. Once that happens, disagreement may still be technically possible while becoming structurally difficult to express.

## Candidate indicators

- one participant's labels become mandatory system fields;
- alternative descriptions are repeatedly translated into the dominant frame;
- disagreement is treated as misunderstanding rather than possible difference;
- stored categories continue to govern after they are contested;
- model outputs reproduce prior ontology despite re-baselining or correction.

## Not the same as

Ontology capture is not ordinary shared vocabulary, domain standardization, or temporary use of a common model for coordination.

## Governance responses

Possible responses include explicit provenance for categories, parallel models, partial translation, disputed state, re-baselining, and preservation of source-language descriptions where materially relevant.

## Open questions

There is no validated universal measure of ontology capture. Future detection must distinguish useful coordination from coercive semantic narrowing.

> A shared language should support relation without silently becoming the only language in which reality is allowed to exist.
