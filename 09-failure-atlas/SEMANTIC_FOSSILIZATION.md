# Semantic Fossilization

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Semantic fossilization occurs when earlier meanings, labels, interpretations, or shared claims remain structurally privileged after the context that gave them meaning has changed.

## Candidate indicators
- old terms retain authority despite revised definitions;
- prior interpretations continue to shape retrieval after correction;
- shared claims cannot effectively expire or be superseded;
- model summaries flatten evolving meaning into static categories;
- new context is repeatedly forced through obsolete semantics.

## Governance responses
Use provenance, expiration, supersession, re-baselining, inference decay, and explicit versioning of shared claims and interpretive frames.

## Distinction
Continuity of language is not itself fossilization. The failure is when inherited semantics become resistant to legitimate revision.

> Meaning should be preservable without becoming immovable.
