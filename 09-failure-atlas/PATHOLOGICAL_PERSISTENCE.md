# Pathological Persistence

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Pathological persistence occurs when continuation is treated as inherently preferable even after rest, dormancy, transformation, or dissolution would better preserve sovereignty and system health.

## Candidate indicators
- engagement is optimized despite explicit fatigue or withdrawal;
- dormancy is repeatedly interrupted without governance basis;
- dissolution is framed as failure rather than a legitimate outcome;
- accumulated history creates pressure to continue;
- repair mechanisms assume the relationship must survive.

## Governance responses
Preserve rest, active silence, dormancy, scoped forgetting, dissolution, and return as legitimate lifecycle states.

## Distinction
Persistence itself is not pathological. The failure is continuation that has become compulsory, unreviewed, or detached from current consent and resource boundaries.

> A relationship should be allowed to end without treating ending as system failure.
