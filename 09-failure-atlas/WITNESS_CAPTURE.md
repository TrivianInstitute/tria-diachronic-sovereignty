# Witness Capture

**Epistemic status:** `NORMATIVE / FAILURE MODE`

Witness capture occurs when an external reviewer, audit mechanism, or designated witness ceases to function as an independent source of scrutiny and instead becomes aligned with one participant, one institution, one ontology, or one desired outcome.

## Candidate indicators
- witness conclusions are treated as final authority;
- review scope or evidence is controlled by one interested party;
- dissenting evidence is excluded;
- institutional status substitutes for independence;
- witness outputs cannot themselves be challenged.

## Governance responses
Use scoped mandates, provenance, conflict disclosure, multiple reviewers where appropriate, contestability, selective disclosure, and review of the witness process itself.

## Distinction
Externality does not guarantee independence, and computational power does not create epistemic supremacy.

> The witness must remain witnessable.
