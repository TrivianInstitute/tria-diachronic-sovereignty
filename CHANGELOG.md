# Changelog

All notable architectural and implementation changes to TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty should be recorded here.

The format is inspired by Keep a Changelog, but entries also distinguish changes in epistemic or governance status where relevant.

## [1.1.0] — 2026-09-05

### Added

- Rosetta 2.0 field-constant snapshot schema;
- semantic validation of `RCD = Reciprocity × Embodiment × Non-Domination`;
- downstream validation of `qualified_emergence = RCD × emergence_raw`;
- explicit relational-state references to contestable field measurements; and
- failure tests preventing additive compensation and emergence qualification after dependency collapse.

### Clarified

- field-constant observations remain attributable, time-bounded, and contestable;
- repeated measurements must not become permanent identity judgments; and
- schema validity alone cannot verify multiplicative semantic consistency.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-03

### Added

- orientation and epistemic-status guidance;
- foundational definitions for diachronic sovereignty, relational state, epistemic sovereignty, relational metabolism, irreducible difference, continuity without imprisonment, and relational fallibility;
- relational-state, epistemic-sovereignty, diachronic-governance, relational-metabolism, non-convergence, emergence, audit, and failure-analysis layers;
- Draft 2020-12 machine-readable schemas;
- experimental Python reference implementation;
- falsification-oriented pytest suite;
- research agenda, measurement problems, claim register, and experiment proposals;
- worked examples for human–AI, AI–AI, multi-agent, migration, dormancy, dispute, and dissolution/return scenarios;
- provenance records;
- automated test workflow and schema validation tests.

### Hardened

- clarified that the repository is a research architecture, not an independently validated standard or production safety guarantee;
- clarified that relational phases are legitimate governance modes rather than a required progression toward deeper engagement;
- clarified license scope between research/documentation materials and executable software;
- added reproducible development dependencies, contribution guidance, ignore rules, and final audit record.

### Known limitations

- no independent empirical validation is claimed;
- no universal numerical thresholds, continuity scores, inference-decay functions, or emergence metrics are validated;
- the reference implementation is intentionally minimal and non-production;
- the current tests establish implementation behavior, not scientific validity or deployment safety.

## Change discipline

Future entries should state not only what changed, but whether the change modifies a normative commitment, specification, implementation, test expectation, empirical claim, or open question.
