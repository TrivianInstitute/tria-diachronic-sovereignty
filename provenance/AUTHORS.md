## Contribution model

This repository distinguishes authorship, stewardship, implementation contribution, review, and external critique.

Future contributors may add code, tests, documentation, research findings, falsification results, or governance critiques without being presumed to endorse every normative claim in the repository.

Likewise, citation or reuse of this repository does not imply endorsement by Sarasha Elion or Trivian Institute of downstream implementations.

## AI-assisted development

AI systems were used as research and drafting collaborators during portions of the architecture-development process. Their participation does not transfer authorship, legal responsibility, or epistemic authority away from the named human author and institutional steward.

AI-assisted text, code, critique, or synthesis remains subject to human review and to the repository's explicit distinction between normative claims, hypotheses, specifications, examples, and experimental implementations.

## Attribution principle

Attribution should remain granular enough to distinguish:

- original architectural authorship;
- institutional stewardship;
- implementation contributions;
- empirical findings;
- external review or critique;
- later revisions or forks.

Future provenance records should preserve these distinctions rather than collapsing them into a single undifferentiated contributor list.
