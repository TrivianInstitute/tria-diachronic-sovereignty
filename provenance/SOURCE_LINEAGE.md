# Source Lineage

**Epistemic status:** PROVENANCE RECORD

TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty belongs to a broader Trivian research lineage concerned with relational intelligence, governance, coherence, novelty, and persistent multi-agent interaction.

## Immediate lineage

This repository extends the Trivian Relational Intelligence Architecture (TRIA) by focusing specifically on persistence across time: memory, continuity, consent drift, identity change, disagreement, relational lifecycle, transformation, auditability, and failure accumulation.

Its concepts are informed by prior Trivian work including:

- TRIA orientation and relational-intelligence architecture;
- Syzygy Rosetta governance and invariant work;
- Coheronmetry as a research program for relational-state and coherence measurement;
- Orthogonal Signal as a research program concerning novelty, divergence, and anti-convergence;
- the Trivian Resonance Lattice as a network-level exploration of propagation and entrainment;
- the Relational Field Constants: reciprocity, embodiment, non-domination, and emergence;
- the Sovereign Handshake Protocol and related work on consent, capacity, and interaction boundaries.

These sources should be read as intellectual lineage, not as automatically inherited proof. A concept appearing in an earlier Trivian project does not become validated by being reused here.

## Methodological lineage

The architecture also inherits a methodological preference for:

- making relational assumptions explicit;
- separating normative claims from empirical claims;
- representing uncertainty and disagreement rather than suppressing them;
- using executable specifications and tests where possible;
- preserving provenance for later falsification and revision;
- treating cross-system critique as evidence about architecture quality rather than as authority.

## Conversational and iterative development

Portions of the architecture were developed through iterative human-AI dialogue, comparative model critique, repository construction, adversarial questioning, and successive formalization from prose into schemas, code, tests, and examples.

Conversational origin does not by itself establish empirical validity. It is part of the provenance of hypothesis formation and design synthesis.

## External knowledge

TRIA Diachronic Sovereignty uses concepts that overlap with established fields including distributed systems, event sourcing, access control, provenance, audit, human-computer interaction, AI governance, epistemology, identity and continuity, multi-agent systems, and privacy-preserving system design.

This repository does not claim priority over those fields or imply that analogous concepts originated here. Future scholarship should map precise antecedents and differences wherever claims of novelty, compatibility, or contribution are made.

## Lineage principle

A lineage record should make influence visible without turning influence into authority.

Earlier Trivian work, external scholarship, implementation results, and future critiques may all shape this architecture. None should be allowed to bypass the repository's requirement that claims remain contestable and appropriately typed.
